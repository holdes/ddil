### Findings

**(a) CSI cable — owned.** Confirmed by the builder. This audit's inventory was
built from Amazon order history, which does not show previously-owned stock, so
treat every "not ordered" line below as "not seen in recent orders" and confirm
before buying.

**(b) No 2.54 mm male header pins anywhere in inventory.** The pHAT breaks out
5V / GND / MOSI / SCK / D7 / D12 / D6 / D16 / D20 / D21 as *bare through-holes*.
The servos terminate in 3-pin **female** connectors, which need male pins to push
onto. Without header strip, every servo and LED wire must be soldered directly and
permanently. **A 40-pin 2.54 mm male header strip (~$5) fixes this.**

**(c) Only one hardware-PWM pin is broken out.** Of the pHAT's exposed GPIO
(D7/D12/D6/D16/D20/D21), only **D12** is hardware-PWM capable — D13/D18/D19 aren't
broken out. So one servo gets hardware PWM and the other needs `pigpio` software
PWM. Fine in practice, worth knowing before wiring.

**(d) Servo STALL current is the real power risk, not running current.** 2× MG90S
at ~700 mA stall = 1.4 A, plus Pi 0.35 A = **1.75 A against a 1.8 A rating.** A wing
catching on a sleeve browns out the Pi. Mitigations: never slew both servos
simultaneously, and fit the **1000 µF bulk caps (not ordered)**.

**(e) Bus conflicts: none.** SPI is used only by the LED strip; I²C only by
BME280 (0x76/77) and the IMU (0x6A/6B); the camera is CSI. Clean.

**(f) PCA9685 is unusable — confirmed geometrically.** 62 × 26 mm fits neither deck
(Deck A's free band between the Pi and the sensors is 22 mm; Deck B can't take it in
either orientation). Servos go direct to GPIO. Not a loss.

## 5. Physical layout fit

**Fixed during this audit: neither custom deck had a cable pass-through.** The OEM
decks each had a ⌀20 hole; I copied their structural interface and dropped it. Every
wire crosses both decks, and the CSI ribbon is **11 mm wide and flat** — a small round
hole would not pass it. Both decks now carry a **22 × 9 mm rounded slot**, collision-
checked against all components.

Otherwise clean:

- Deck A: 4 components, **47 %** used, 0 collisions
- Deck B: 3 components, **34 %** used, 0 collisions
- Vertical: 92.0 mm used of 95.10 mm available
- Camera headroom above Deck B: 57.10 mm for a 48.0 bracket
