# 08 — Pinout reference

Generated by `cad/gen_pinout.py`. **Marked CONFIRM = verify on your own board before soldering.** The 40-pin header is a fixed standard; the pHAT breakout set and every I²C address depend on what is actually in the box.

⚠ **Pins 27 and 28 (ID_SD / ID_SC) are the HAT EEPROM bus.** Do not use them for anything.

## Power rules

- **One cell, one 5 V rail.** No BEC, no second supply. Everything shares it.
- **Never slew both servos at once** — 2× MG90S stall ≈ 1.4 A on top of the Pi's 0.35 A against a 1.8 A supply.
- **Cap LED brightness at ~30%** — 12 LEDs at full white is 0.72 A.
- **1000 µF bulk caps across 5 V**, close to the servo connectors.
- **Common ground everywhere.** A floating ground on the LED strip produces symptoms that look exactly like a software bug.
