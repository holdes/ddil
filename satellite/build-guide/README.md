# 1U CubeSat Demo — Build Guide

Elastic × Aerospace groundstation demo. A 1U CubeSat prop that feeds **real**
telemetry into the existing OpenC3 COSMOS / Zeek / Elastic stack, with two
audience-facing wow factors: commandable servo-deployed wings, and LEDs that
light on IDS alerts.

**Status:** Phase 1 — no solar hardware, no GPS. Both are additive later and the
CAD already reserves space and routing for them.

---

## Read this first

Three things in this build are **not yet verified**, and two of them will stop
you. They are listed in [`OPEN-ITEMS.md`](OPEN-ITEMS.md) with what needs
checking. The relevant guide sections carry a **STOP** gate at the point where
each one bites.

Short version:

| # | Item | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Frame posts have **no panel fixing holes**, and the X/Y face patterns collide at the post centre | Panel mounting (§03) |
| 2 | Post mount plate's 2× M3 may land on base-plate perforations | Printing the post (§05) |
| 3 | Qwiic pHAT may cover the GPIO header the APA102 needs | LED wiring (§04) |

Everything else has been measured off the meshes and checked.

## Conventions

- **Dimensions are millimetres.** Where a guide quotes one, the sheet it came
  from is named.
- ☐ Checkbox steps are sequential within a section.
- **STOP** means do not proceed past this point until the named item is resolved.
- ⚠ marks a step where the common mistake is expensive (wrong part, wrong
  orientation, something that traps a component inside the assembly).
- Where a value is *assumed* rather than measured, it says so inline. Do not
  quietly promote an assumption to a fact.

## Coordinate frame

Satellite-local, origin at the body centre, matching the CAD:

| Axis | Direction | What's on it |
|---|---|---|
| **+X / −X** | port / starboard | wing mount panels, servos, wings |
| **+Y** | aft | ports panel — microSD, USB, harness exit |
| **−Y** | forward | camera panel — lens bore |
| **+Z** | up | top plate |
| **−Z** | down | base plate, pedestal post mounts here |

On the pedestal the whole body leans **20° about X**, tipping −Y (the camera
face) upward. That is deliberate: it points the lens at standing eye height and
reads as "spacecraft on orbit" rather than "box on a stick".

## Tools

Nothing exotic. The build is deliberately solderless.

**Required**

- Soldering iron with **heat-set insert tips** (M3). A conical tip will work but
  goes in crooked; the insert tips are ~$6 and worth it
- Drill or pin vice + **4.1 mm** bit (insert holes) and **3.4 mm** bit (clearance)
- 2.0 mm and 2.5 mm hex drivers, small Phillips
- Flush cutters, tweezers
- Digital calipers — several steps say "measure the real part", and they mean it
- Multimeter (continuity + voltage; used once, at first power-on, and it matters)

**Not required:** no soldering for sensors, no crimping, no hot glue.

## Time

Print time dominates and depends on your machine. Assembly, once everything is
printed and delivered, is roughly:

| Stage | Time |
|---|---|
| Heat-set inserts (16+ of them) | 45 min |
| Frame + deck stack | 30 min |
| Electronics, I²C chain | 45 min |
| Wings + servo calibration | 60 min |
| Pedestal + ballast | 20 min |
| Software bring-up + checkout | 90 min |

Budget a full day if it is your first pass, and do the servo calibration
(§05) before you glue or commit anything — it is the step most likely to need
a reprint.
