Power-on

What you should see after master power.

1
PDU rocker glows red
Everything on the PDU powers up together.
2
JetKVM standard screen
IP appears once Ethernet links. USB and HDMI read Disconnected until the Framework is up.
3
UniFi reads "Plug in Cable"
Expected. No WAN uplink in DDIL operation; the kit network still runs.
4
Switch blue status LED
Next to its USB-C power lead.
5
Touchscreen: Boot Diagnostics, then Idle
Probe log for six services, then the branding page with a QR code and a diagnostics page.
The kiosk health is mockedMOCK = true in demo/kiosk/src/lib/health.ts. A green board does not prove the services are up; a random FAIL is noise. Use the runbook 02 checks. The Jina server needs a manual start after every boot.
